Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

EntaBrainPay is a quiz application built by EntaChain. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what your rights are.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: if you sign in with email — your email address and password (stored hashed by Firebase Authentication). You can also play as a guest without an email.
  • Profile: player name (optional), app language, selected theme.
  • Gameplay: answers, scores, coins, XP, level, streaks, tournament participation and rankings.
  • PayPal email: only if you request a payout from a sponsored tournament.
  • Technical data: device identifier and crash information needed for ads and stability.

2. Why we use it

  • to run the game — questions, scores, rankings, coins;
  • to prevent cheating and abuse (server-side scoring);
  • to show ads that fund the app;
  • to pay out sponsored tournament prizes.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier to serve ads. Read more in Google's policy. You can limit personalisation in Android settings (Google → Ads).

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We use only the providers needed to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions) — account and gameplay storage;
  • Google AdMob — advertising;
  • PayPal — only when you request a payout.

5. Storage and security

Data lives in Google Cloud (us-central1) and is transferred encrypted. Server-side rules keep clients from changing balances, levels or scores.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, including full account deletion. Write to entachain@gmail.com — we answer within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page.
