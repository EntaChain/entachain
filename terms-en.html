Terms of Use

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. The game

EntaBrainPay is a free quiz game. You answer questions, collect coins and XP, and level up. There is no betting, no casino mechanics and no paid entry.

2. Coins

Coins are the in-game currency. They are not money, cannot be transferred between players and cannot be exchanged for cash outside sponsored tournaments.

3. Daily tournament

  • opens automatically every day at 00:05 (Europe/Sofia);
  • entry is free — just tap “Join”;
  • ranking is by the number of correct answers that day;
  • only answers given after joining the tournament count.

4. Sponsored tournaments and prizes

When a business sponsors a tournament, the prize pool is split: 50% for first place, 20% for second and 10% for third; 20% stays with the platform. Payouts are made via PayPal, minimum €0.50.

Prizes are paid only when there is a real sponsored pool — ordinary play never guarantees a cash win.

5. Fair play

Automation, bots, device manipulation or any attempt to alter results are prohibited. In case of a violation the account may be restricted or banned and any prize forfeited.

6. Account

You can play as a guest or link your account to an email address. You are responsible for keeping your password secure.

7. Changes

We may update these terms; material changes will be published on this page.
