Frequently asked

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads. There are no entry fees and no deposits.

Can I win money?

Cash prizes exist only in sponsored tournaments, when a business funds the prize pool. Payouts go through PayPal with a minimum of €0.50.

Is there gambling?

No. There are no stakes, no casino mechanics and no paid entry. Your result depends only on correct answers.

How do I join the daily tournament?

Open the “Daily tournament” card on the home screen, tap “Join” and play quizzes. The ranking counts correct answers for the day.

Which languages are supported?

Bulgarian, English and German, each with its own question bank.
