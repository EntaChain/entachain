Delete your EntaBrainPay account

This page explains how to request deletion of your account in EntaBrainPay (publisher EntaChain) and which data is deleted or retained.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address you use in the app to entachain@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Delete EntaBrainPay account.
  3. Include your player name or the email you play with (mention if you play as a guest).
  4. You will receive confirmation within 3 business days and deletion within 30 days.

Data that is deleted

  • email address and sign-in data (if you have an account);
  • player name, language and theme settings;
  • gameplay data: answers, scores, coins, XP, level, streaks;
  • tournament participation and leaderboard entries;
  • saved PayPal email used for payouts;
  • reports you filed about other players (kept anonymised for moderation).

Data that is retained, and for how long

  • Payout and accounting records — kept for 5 years in anonymised form, because they are accounting documents required by law.
  • Technical logs and crash data — up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Anonymous statistics (total games, average scores) — not linked to any person.

Deleting part of your data

You can change your player name at any time in Profile → Statistics → pencil next to the name. To remove specific data without deleting the account, email us and we will handle it.
